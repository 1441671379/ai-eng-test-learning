# Recorded word pronunciation pack

Source recordings are individually credited in ATTRIBUTION.json. They originate
from Wikimedia Commons and are indexed through English Wiktionary data supplied
by Kaikki / Wiktextract. The index source URL, retrieval metadata and checksum
are recorded in manifest.json.

The extracted word/pronunciation mapping (including part-of-speech and IPA
metadata) is adapted from English Wiktionary contributors via Kaikki. That
derived mapping is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Source articles and their
contributor histories are available at https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/{word};
the original snapshot and its checksum are listed in manifest.json. Audio files
continue to use their own individual licences rather than this mapping licence.
See https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights and
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

Each recording keeps its own licence; there is no blanket licence replacing the
original grants. ATTRIBUTION.json contains the file title, author, source page,
licence name and URL, any additional attribution/credit supplied by Commons,
checksum and processing notice. Preserve this file when redistributing the pack.
The application also exposes the attribution for the currently selected audio.

Only recordings explicitly marked Public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA are
selected by this builder. The licence stated for each recording continues to
apply to that recording. This pack does not contain proprietary dictionary audio
fetched through unofficial dictionary endpoints.

Original OGG recordings are retained. Where the original was a WAV or another
format, the Wikimedia-provided OGG rendition is used and identified in its
processing notice. This build does not trim recordings or change their volume.
Availability of a recording does not guarantee a specific accent, consistent
recording quality, or the intended pronunciation of every heteronym.
