# English lexical pronunciations

This catalog contains word identity, display labels and explicit ARPAbet readings
used for pronunciation selection. It contains no recordings, model weights,
personal vocabulary, learner recognition results or learning history.

The original readings were prepared from the application's wordbooks, English
pronunciation dictionaries and manually selected lexical alternatives. CMUdict's
notice is retained in LICENSE.CMUDICT.txt; wordbook provenance remains alongside
the installed wordbooks. These are synthesis instructions, not sounds recognized
from a learner's recording. Kokoro generates and caches audio locally on demand.
